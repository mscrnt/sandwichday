Event Details

What to Expect

Join us for a Scott Pilgrim vs. The World screening and a build-your-own sandwich bar! We're hanging out from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and the movie starts at 6:00 PM. Come early, build a sandwich, and hang out before the show. Good vibes and great company guaranteed!

🍺

What to Bring

We're providing the bread, spreads, and toppings for build-your-own sandwiches, plus limited drinks. Feel free to bring your favorite beverages and any sides you'd like to contribute to make the feast even better!

Join the Discord

Chat with other party-goers, share what you're bringing, and post photos!

🥪 The Food 🥪

Build Your Own Sandwich

BYOS! Pick your bread, pile on your favorites, and make it exactly how you like it.

Breads

A few different breads and rolls to build on, including a gluten-free option

Spreads

Multiple spreads and sauces to choose from, plus peanut butter & jelly for the PB&J crowd

Toppings

Deli meats like turkey, ham, and salami, a few kinds of cheese, and fresh veggies: lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, peppers, and more

Plus beverages and more!

🥜 Heads up: peanut butter will be on the table. Let us know about any allergies!
